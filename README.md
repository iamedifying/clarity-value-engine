# Clarity Value Engine

A data-driven fundamental research engine inspired by the investment principles of Benjamin Graham and Warren Buffett.

## Project Overview

Clarity Value Engine is a data science project designed to analyse companies from multiple perspectives rather than relying on a single financial metric.

The system will combine:

- Company fundamentals
- Industry-relative analysis
- Macroeconomic conditions
- Intrinsic valuation
- Relative valuation
- Margin of safety
- Scenario and sensitivity analysis
- Industry trend detection
- Emerging investment-opportunity research
- Historical backtesting
- Machine learning

## Core Research Questions

The project will investigate five major questions:

1. **Business:** How financially strong and economically attractive is the company?
2. **Industry:** How does the company compare with its industry peers?
3. **Environment:** What external economic and industry factors may affect the company?
4. **Valuation:** What range of values could reasonably be assigned to the company under different assumptions?
5. **Opportunity:** Are there industries displaying emerging characteristics that warrant further research?

## Philosophy

The system is designed as a research and analytical tool, not an automated buy/sell system.

Its objective is to transform financial and economic data into structured evidence that can support further human analysis.
